# Prizm UX review — 2026-11-15

Walkthrough of prizmproject.vercel.app (production), desktop and 375px phone width,
both modes (Sportsbook, Markets), every tab. Notes are what a first-time visitor
would hit, ranked by how much they hurt. Research notes and sources at the end.

## The one-line read

The data is honest and rich, but the pages talk in the model's internal language,
lead with the wrong numbers, and contradict themselves in a few places. A new
visitor can't tell in five seconds what Prizm thinks they should look at today, or
how much to trust it.

## P0 — bugs and contradictions (fix first, mostly small)

1. **Desktop header overflows.** At a 1024px-wide window the page is 1281px wide
   (search box + mode toggle push past the edge), so every page gets a horizontal
   scrollbar and the Markets toggle is cut off. Phone width is fine.
2. ~~Missing space on Home "214/44149%"~~ — false alarm: a text-extraction artifact;
   the page renders "214/441 49%" correctly.
3. **LEAN is still shown** (Home 7-day table column, Performance tier card and
   "Hit rate by label"), though the tier was removed in June. Either drop it or label
   it "retired tier".
4. **Internal tuning advice is on a public page:** Performance shows "Suggest -7pt
   adj (current: -3)". That's a note for us, not users.
5. **Edge contradicts the tiers.** The top "+EV" rows are mostly FADE-tier
   (Dyson Daniels +26.9% EV, FADE). A user sees "best bet" and "don't take this" on
   the same row. Pick one story: hide FADE rows from Edge, or explain the
   difference in one sentence on the page.
6. **Trends headings disagree with rows:** "10 for 10" group shows "12 in a row";
   hot-streak rows carry FADE badges with no explanation.
7. **Home "Top props" sort looks wrong:** 59 PLAY rows sit above 60/61 LOCK rows.
8. **Feed timestamps disagree:** "Updated at 11 AM ET" banner on parlays "Posted
   3:11 PM ET"; the Prizm Update card repeats the same sentence four times.
9. **Developer page in the main nav:** "Status" (pipeline ✗/✓ grid) is in the top
   nav for every visitor. Move to a footer link or `/status` only.
10. **Placeholder jargon:** Markets Trends says "Coming in phase 3." Users don't
    know our phases.

## P1 — what the pages lead with (biggest UX win)

- **Home leads with "12 LOCK / 23 PLAY / 375 FADE".** The biggest, reddest number
  is the one we tell people to ignore. Lead with today's picks (LOCK/PLAY), not
  the count of non-picks; show FADE as a quiet "375 other props scored".
- **The headline record is the wrong record.** Home "Yesterday overall 49%" and
  Performance "Overall 48%" average every scored prop including FADE, so the first
  number a visitor sees says "coin flip". The number that matters is the LOCK/PLAY
  record with its sample size (e.g. "LOCK 61% · 492 graded"), shown next to the
  honest caveat already on Edge (−5% ROI at real odds on held-out data).
- **Small samples shouted as results:** Markets Home shows "1-3 · ROI −53.3% on 4
  graded" in red as the headline. Under ~30 graded, say "Too early to judge —
  4 graded so far" and show the count, not a percentage.
- **Calibration table is mostly empty rows** (65–85+ all "0 props"). Hide empty
  bands or widen the window until bands have data.
- **One idea per screen.** Home currently mixes slate, tier counts, top props,
  how-it-works, and a 7-day table. Suggested order: today's best picks → record
  that backs them (LOCK/PLAY, with n) → link to everything else.

## P2 — language (match the user's words, one term per idea)

- **Unexplained badges and abbreviations:** EXP, STARTER, COUNTER, L10, H2H,
  "Books overprice −39%", "2 alts", "safer/riskier", 73L / 49F. Add a small
  legend or a tooltip on first use; spell out once ("L10 = last 10 games").
- **Three names for one thing:** "Performance" (desktop nav) vs "Stats" (phone
  nav) vs "Model Performance" (Home section). Pick one.
- **Three team formats:** "DAL vs BOS" (Home), "Dallas Mavericks @ Boston Celtics"
  (Props filter), "DAL @ BOS" (Markets). Use "DAL @ BOS" everywhere (away @ home).
- **Two date formats:** "Safe Pick · 2026-11-15" vs "Nov 15". Use "Nov 15".
- **Gambling framing vs forecasting:** parlay names "High Roller", "Jackpot",
  "~18x payout" and a "Daily Streak" counter are the patterns gambling-UX research
  flags as dark nudges. If Prizm is a forecasting tool, name them by size
  ("2-leg", "5-leg") and show the model's combined probability next to the payout.
- **Score scale ambiguity:** the badge number (59, 60, 73) is the raw score, while
  Markets shows calibrated "Model 58%". Show one scale to users — the calibrated %
  ("hits ~61% historically") reads better than a 0–100 score.

## P3 — phone (375px)

- **Props: filters fill the whole first screen;** no prop is visible until you
  scroll past four filter groups and five full team-name chips. Put filters behind
  one "Filters (2)" button opening a bottom sheet, with active filters as removable
  chips above results.
- **Markets ladder tables scroll sideways inside each card** (L5 / L10 / Season are
  off-screen). Keep 3 priority columns (Rung, Price, Hit rate) and tap a rung to
  expand the rest.
- **Bottom nav has 6 items** (Home, Feed, Edge, Trends, Props, Stats); 5 is the
  usual ceiling. Trends could fold into Props as a filter/sort ("on a streak").
- Home slate chips scroll horizontally with no visible affordance beyond a partly
  cut-off chip — fine, but add edge fade so it reads as scrollable.

## P4 — empty and in-progress states

- Markets Edge: three sections all say "The study has not run yet." Good honesty;
  add why and when ("waiting on last season's data backfill; runs automatically
  when complete") and one link to something useful today (Kalshi ladders).
- Feed jumps from today to Nov 8 with nothing in between — say "no parlays
  posted Nov 9–14" or don't show the gap.

## Suggested order of work

1. P0 items 1–4, 9, 10 (an afternoon: CSS overflow, copy, removing LEAN/admin bits).
2. Home + Performance headline rework (P1): picks first, LOCK/PLAY record with n,
   min-sample rule for small records.
3. Terminology pass (P2): one glossary, one team/date format, tooltips.
4. Phone filters bottom sheet + ladder priority columns (P3).
5. Edge/Trends consistency rules (P0 5–6) — needs a decision on what FADE means
   on those pages.

## Research notes

- **Glanceability and hierarchy:** dashboards are read in glances; put the most
  important number top-left and reserve color for meaning. Prizm's biggest
  colored number (375 FADE, red) is the least important. ([Made Good][mg],
  [UX Studio][uxs], [Domo][domo])
- **Progressive disclosure:** show the essential first, reveal detail on demand.
  Fits the Props "reason" chips and the Markets ladder columns.
  ([Decision Lab][dl], [UXPin][uxpin])
- **Mobile filters:** put filters in their own layer (bottom sheet / full screen)
  behind a labeled button; keep applied filters visible as removable chips.
  ([Baymard][baymard])
- **Mobile tables:** pick priority columns or stacked cards; past ~6 columns,
  sideways scroll hides what matters. ([sizzy][sizzy], [Locofy][locofy])
- **Communicating uncertainty:** show track record and sample size by default;
  frequency framing ("hits about 6 in 10") is easier to read than a score; a big
  headline probability drives over-trust. ([Hullman, PCAST][hullman],
  [Gelman blog][gelman], [Yang et al. CHI 2024][dice])
- **Speak the user's language, one term per idea** (Nielsen heuristic 2).
  ([NN/g][nng])
- **Empty states:** say why it's empty, what will fill it, and give a next step.
  ([Atlassian][atl], [SAP Fiori][sap])
- **Gambling dark patterns to avoid:** streak rewards, "jackpot" framing,
  payout-first displays, hidden risk info. ([Newall 2025][newall],
  [Springer heuristic study][springer], [Scientific American][sciam])
